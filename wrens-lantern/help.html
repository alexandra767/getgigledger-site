Wren's Lantern

Wren's Lantern: Help

How do I play?

Drag anywhere to move. Wren throws sparks by herself at the nearest enemy inside her light. Flick quickly to roll; nothing can hurt you mid-roll. When the round button glows, tap it for a burst.

It is too hard

On the title screen open Settings and set Play style to Relaxed: two more hearts, fewer and slower enemies, and a heal in every chest.

How do I pause or leave a run?

Tap the pause button in the bottom left corner. Tap anywhere to resume, or tap Quit to menu. Your score and gold are kept.

What are the cracks in the walls?

Secret doors. A crack only shows when your light reaches it. Walk up to it and it opens. When the room is cleared and the gate is open, step into the door. Inside you choose one: take the gold, or wake the keeper and fight it for a piece of rare gear. The room's gate then leads on to the next room.

Can I hide from enemy shots?

Yes. Stone pillars stop the glowing shots that enemies and bosses fire. Barrels and crates do not.

What is Boss Rush?

Every boss, back to back. It unlocks when you beat your first boss. You start in an armoury and pick five upgrades, then get five more and three hearts back after each boss. It keeps its own scores.

What are Quests and the Journal?

Each hero has three quests, which only count while you play as that hero. Finishing all three earns that hero's flame, which you can wear from Looks. The Journal keeps each line of the story as you come across it.

Can I rename the spirit?

Yes. Open Spirit on the title screen and tap its name to give it the next name on the list.

Where is my gear?

Buy gear in the Store with gold. Everything you own is in your Bag: tap a piece to wear it, tap it again to take it off.

How do I change the volume or turn off vibration?

Settings on the title screen has music volume, sound effects volume and vibration.

The game runs slowly on my phone

In Settings set Graphics to Lite.

What are "Anonymous stats"?

The game sends anonymous information (for example that a run ended at room 7, or that the last session crashed) so problems can be fixed. Nothing personal is sent. Turn it off in Settings on the title screen.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. On iPhone and iPad (version 1.5 and later) it is also kept in your own iCloud, so it comes back after a reinstall or on a new device signed in to the same Apple account. On Android it is saved on the phone only, and deleting the game deletes it.

Does my progress move between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes, from version 1.5. Your gold, gear, level, scores and achievements are kept in your own iCloud and added together across your devices. Open the game on the other device and it catches up, usually within a minute. Your settings and a run in progress stay on each device. You can turn this off under Settings on the title screen (iCloud sync). Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple account with iCloud on.

Do I lose my run if the game closes?

No. A story run is kept at the start of each room from room 2 on. If the game is closed, or you pause and choose Quit to menu, tap Play (it reads Continue) and pick Continue to return to the start of the room you were in, with your hearts, upgrades and score. Pick New run from room 1 to begin again; the score of the run you leave still goes on your board. Your level, gold and gear are always kept either way. Daily, weekly and Boss Rush runs are not kept.

Where do I see what my hero is wearing?

Tap Gear on the title screen. Your hero stands between ten places gear is worn: lantern, necklace, cloak, armour and gloves on one side; charm, left ring, right ring, legs and boots on the other. Under them is what it all adds up to: hearts, spark damage, move speed and chest gold.

How do I put gear on or take it off?

On the Gear screen tap a place. Your Bag opens on that kind of gear: tap a piece to wear it, tap a worn piece to take it off (the lantern always stays on). To put a ring on a particular hand, tap that hand's place first. You can wear two different rings, one on each hand. New gear is bought in the Store with gold you earn by playing, and bosses and chests drop pieces too.

What do the gear colours and sets mean?

Gear is Common, Rare, Epic or Legendary, shown by the colour of its name. Some pieces belong to a set, named on the piece. Wear three pieces of the same set for its bonus. The Gear screen shows each set you have started and how many pieces you are wearing. Worn gear also shows on your hero.

Can I start a run further in?

Yes. Once you beat a world's boss, Play asks where to begin: room 1, or the first room of any world you have reached. Starting further in gives you five upgrades to pick for each world skipped.

Why do more enemies arrive after I clear a room?

After the first three rooms, enemies come in waves. When "MORE ARE COMING" shows, the room is not over yet. The chest appears after the last wave.

Why did a chest give gold but no upgrade?

After the first world, some ordinary rooms pay gold only. Elite rooms, bosses, the merchant and the cursed chest always bring an upgrade.

Does strong gear make the game easy?

No. Enemies are tougher against strong gear, so good gear keeps you safer but never makes a room a walkover. You can hold 8 hearts at most, and gear and upgrades give one free hit a room at most.

What are the merchant and the cursed chest?

In each world of a story run, room 5 has a merchant who sells one upgrade for gold, and room 8 has a cursed chest that gives power at a price (for example, harder-hitting sparks for one fewer heart). A curse lasts for that run only. You can always walk on without taking anything.

What is a hero's signature?

Each hero's Lantern Burst does something extra. Wren is safe from harm for 2 seconds. Pip's dodge is ready again at once. Bram's burst hits for +2 and throws enemies twice as far.

What happens after the last world?

There are eight worlds, each ending in a boss. After the last one the run goes round again as a new lap. Every boss returns changed: a new name and colour, more attacks and a second signature move. The first time you beat a changed boss it leaves a Mythic piece of gear, which cannot be bought in the Store.

Where are my trophies and stats?

Tap Trophies on the title screen for your lifetime record, your best score this week, and a trophy for each boss you have beaten. The first win over each boss pays 100 gold.

Can I change my gear in the middle of a run?

Yes. Pause, Quit to menu, tap Gear (or the Store) and change what you wear, then tap Continue. The run picks up with the gear you have on now.

Is the first run easier?

Yes. Your very first run has fewer enemies in the first three rooms, and if you fall you are brought back once on full hearts ("Rekindled"). After that it is the full game. Relaxed mode in Settings stays available for an easier time on every run.

Why did upgrade cards appear in the middle of a room?

That is the early pick: three kills into a run you choose your first upgrade. The fight waits while you choose. It comes once a run, and not in the daily, weekly or boss rush.

What is the daily streak?

Finish the daily challenge on days in a row and it pays more gold: +25 on the second day, growing to +150 from the seventh day on. The Daily button shows your streak and what today is worth. Missing a day starts it again.

What is the line under my score?

It shows the nearest thing you have still to earn, such as how many levels until the next hero.

What is the "What's new" card?

After an update, the title screen shows once what changed. Tap Got it to close it.

How do I rate the game?

On the title screen tap Help, then Rate the game at the bottom. It opens the store page, where you can leave stars or a few words. The same button shows on the results screen after you beat a boss.
