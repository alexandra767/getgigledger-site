Wren's Lantern: Help

How do I play?

Drag anywhere to move. Wren throws sparks by herself at the nearest enemy inside her light. Flick quickly to roll; nothing can hurt you mid-roll. When the round button glows, tap it for a burst.

It is too hard

On the title screen open Settings and set Play style to Relaxed: two more hearts, fewer and slower enemies, and a heal in every chest.

How do I pause or leave a run?

Tap the pause button in the bottom left corner. Tap anywhere to resume, or tap Quit to menu. Your score and gold are kept.

Where is my gear?

Buy gear in the Store with gold. Everything you own is in your Bag: tap a piece to wear it, tap it again to take it off.

How do I change the volume or turn off vibration?

Settings on the title screen has music volume, sound effects volume and vibration.

The game runs slowly on my phone

In Settings set Graphics to Lite.

What are "Anonymous stats"?

The game sends anonymous information (for example that a run ended at room 7, or that the last session crashed) so problems can be fixed. Nothing personal is sent. Turn it off in Settings on the title screen.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your phone only. Deleting the game deletes it.
