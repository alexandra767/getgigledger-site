Wren's Lantern: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

The short version

Wren's Lantern does not collect your name, email, location, contacts or anything else that says who you are. It sends a small amount of anonymous information about how the game is running so crashes and problems can be found and fixed. You can turn that off.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, gold, gear, level and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

Anonymous stats

The game sends anonymous events through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. An event says things like: the game was opened, a run started or ended and at which room, a boss was beaten, the game switched to lighter graphics, or the last session ended in a crash. Each event carries the game version, the phone model and system, and a random code made on your phone. That code is scrambled before it is sent and cannot be turned back into you. No advertising ID is used and nothing is shared with advertisers.

To turn this off: open Settings on the title screen and set Anonymous stats to OFF.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No purchases
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change reaches the game.
