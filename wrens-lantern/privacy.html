Wren's Lantern: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

The short version

Wren's Lantern does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, gold, gear, level, settings and daily challenge results are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No purchases
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Children

Because the game collects nothing, it collects nothing from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change reaches the game.

Questions: support@gigledger.org
