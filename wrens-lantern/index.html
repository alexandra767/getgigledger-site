Wren'sLantern

A small knight, a big lantern, and a very dark dungeon.

Coming to iPhone and Android. Free, with no ads and nothing to buy. Friends are testing it now. To join them, write to support@gigledger.org.

Only what you light can be hit

Wren throws sparks by herself at anything inside her light. Land hits and the light grows. Get hurt and it shrinks, and the monsters outside it are safe from you. So you keep it burning.

Drag to move
One thumb, anywhere on the screen.
Flick to roll
Nothing can hurt you in the middle of a roll.
Tap to burst
When the round button glows, one tap clears the space around you.

Five worlds down

Each world is ten rooms, and the tenth has someone waiting in it. Clear a room and a chest opens: gold, sometimes new gear, and a pick of three upgrades that stack until the run ends.

What is in the dungeon

  • 5bosses, one at the bottom of each world
  • 9kinds of monsters that each need a different answer
  • 22upgrades to mix into a new build every run
  • 40pieces of gear to buy with gold you earn: lanterns, armour, boots and charms
  • 2heroes. Wren is steady. Pip the fox is quicker on the dodge.
  • 1daily challenge, with its own twist and a gold reward

Not that coordinated? Turn on the Relaxed play style in Settings for more hearts, fewer monsters and a heal in every chest.

No ads. Nothing to buy. No account. No signal needed.

Gold comes from playing and from nowhere else. Your progress stays on your phone. Runs are short, and there is always one more room.
